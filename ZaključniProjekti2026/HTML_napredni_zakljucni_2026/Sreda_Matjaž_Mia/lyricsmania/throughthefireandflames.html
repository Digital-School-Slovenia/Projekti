About


“Through the Fire and Flames” is DragonForce’s most popular song to date. It became notorious for being the most difficult song featured in the rhythm game Guitar Hero III: Legends of Rock, and later on Guitar Hero Smash Hits. It would also go on to appear as downloadable content for Rock Band 3 and Rocksmith 2014, and was featured on the soundtrack for the video game Brütal Legend.
