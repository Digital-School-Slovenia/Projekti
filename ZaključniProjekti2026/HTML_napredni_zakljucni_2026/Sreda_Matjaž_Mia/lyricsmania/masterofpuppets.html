About


The title track off Metallica’s seminal Master of Puppets album is about the negative effects drugs can have on a person’s life, written from the perspective of the drug itself. The song highlights how drugs control the person’s life, how they take over every aspect. The person becomes unable to think clearly, unable to make their own choices and unable to stop the abuse as the drug always demands more.

“Master of Puppets” is the single most performed song in Metallica’s career, and always provides a great reaction from audiences, as Hetfield will leave the singing of the “MASTER!” exclamations to the audience (to great effect!). The guitar riff between the last guitar solo section and final verse was heavily influenced by David Bowie’s song “Andy Warhol,” which Burton and Hammett were big fans of.

This song later gets a resurgence in popularity as in the Stranger Things season four, episode “The Piggyback”, Eddie Munson plays the first half of the song to distract bat-like creatures to give his friends time to kill Vecna: the mind warping antagonist of the series whose original idea was Dungeons and Dragon’s undead Lich.
